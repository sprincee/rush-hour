# Gameplay plan: make lessons 1-4 great to play (2026-11-13)

From a game-design audit of lessons 1-4. The goal is to make Group A great to play and to teach well,
then reuse the same patterns in every later lesson. Nothing here is built yet. Decide
each item with the owner before building.

Ranked by value to the player:

1. **Built 2026-11-16 for lesson 2** (four moments); lessons 1, 3 and 4 still need theirs. Includes #7 (moments flip the X-ray on). **Freeze frames on first events.** The first time something meaningful happens (first ticket on the rail,
   first turn-away, first batch), pause briefly with one callout in shop words plus the software term.
   Once per lesson, can be switched off. An HTML overlay, so the frozen canvas drawing doesn't change.
2. **Built 2026-11-16, in every lesson.** **Live goal meters during the rush.** "Longest wait 12.3 / 15 min", warming in color as it nears the
   limit, and live `ON TIME / AT RISK / MISSED` badges (already specified in DESIGN_RH.md, not built).
3. **Built 2026-11-16, in every level** ("Your call", with a ★ for calling it). **Predict before running.** One tap-to-answer question before "Run the rush". The debrief compares
   the guess with what happened.
4. **Built 2026-11-16, being tuned in lesson 2:** the debrief payoff (receipt prints, counts up, stamp) and sound. **Shorter debrief, lesson first.** Verdict, one bold "what happened" sentence, and the next action on top.
   Receipt numbers and the concept card fold away. The SLO note shows once per lesson.
5. **Queue chart in the debrief, with a ghost of the previous run** so before and after can be compared.
6. ~~Canvas readability on phones.~~ Checked on the owner's phone on 2026-11-16: readable. Dropped.
7. **The X-ray as part of play.** Auto-flip it at a key moment in each lesson's first level, with a callout.
8. **Stars built 2026-11-16, in every level; saving them waits for accounts.** **Saved progress and three stars per level:** goals met, under budget, and a stretch goal.

Smaller cleanups:
- Done 2026-11-16: collapse the four locked-part cards on every brief to one line ("Coming later").
- Done 2026-11-16: make jargon (503, utilization, latency) tap-to-define.

Polish built 2026-11-16: sounds of the shop during the rush, effects over the shop, and every input reacting (see DESIGN_RH.md, Motion and Sound).

Still to build: #5 (queue chart in the debrief, with a ghost of the last run), freeze frames for lessons 1, 3 and 4, and optionally a "real cluster" line in the debrief from the recordings.

Suggested start: items 1, 2 and 4. The owner also has their own UI ideas to bring; hear those first.
